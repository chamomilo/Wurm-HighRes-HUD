# Settings

Open Main menu > Mod updates > High-res HUD > Settings. Each parameter has hover help. Valid edits are saved automatically and applied during play where supported; startup-only changes are identified in the help. The shared language selector controls the settings window.
Font size controls window contents only, including inventory rows, chat and event text; headings retain their own sizes. 0 follows the native font preference.

Default opacity of inventory windows accepts whole percentages from 10 to 100,
with 100 as the initial default. All high-res windows use this setting. Windows
with title controls display opacity as the fourth frameless 22 px square,
immediately left of Minimize, Maximize and Close. Drag its vertical marker left
or right; the area
to its left is green. Opacity affects the entire window, including text and
frames. The slider stays fully visible, including at 10 percent. Reopening a
window restores the configured default; changing that default keeps already
open windows at their individual values.
Chat and Events keep their slider beside the tab strip. The title square also
stays available while its window is collapsed. Hover displays its percentage.
Their adjacent Compact Minimize button collapses the window to its channel
strip and docks it at the lower screen edge. A tab click or Restore returns
the previous expanded bounds; channel history and entered commands are retained.

A saved change that requires a client restart immediately shows a warning. Continue configuring keeps the game running; Exit game first saves valid pending edits and opens Wurm's normal quit confirmation. An exit offer remains in the Settings footer while restart changes are pending.
